Privacy Policy

How Seva24 collects, protects, and handles your personal information.

Last Updated: September 2026

Your privacy is essential to us. This Privacy Policy details the information collected by Seva24 ("we", "our", or "us"), an application owned and operated by DGTALIZE PARTNERS Co., Ltd. (developer of Seva24, package: com.renobuild.mobile.app) when you use our mobile application and home service booking services in Cambodia, and your rights regarding your data.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide seamless, reliable service booking between customers and vetted professionals:

  • Account & Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and optional email address when registering or requesting a booking.
  • Service Location & Address: Address and approximate or precise location coordinates required for technicians to reach your service site (e.g. for aircon repair, plumbing, renovations).
  • Booking Information: Service type, preferred appointment date/time, descriptions, and optional photos you upload illustrating the repair need.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, app version, and basic analytics to troubleshoot technical issues and optimize app performance.

2 App Permissions We Request

Depending on your usage, our mobile application may request the following system permissions with your explicit consent:

  • Location (Fine / Coarse): To auto-detect your service address and match nearby available technicians. Location is only accessed when actively using location-based features.
  • Camera / Photos: To allow you to capture or upload photos of issues requiring maintenance or repair.
  • Notifications: To send you live updates on technician arrival, booking confirmation, and status alerts.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Facilitating and confirming service bookings with verified service professionals.
  • Enabling communication between you and the assigned technician regarding job arrival and scope.
  • Providing responsive customer support and dispute resolution via phone, messaging, or email.
  • Ensuring platform safety, fraud prevention, and verifying service quality.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared under these limited circumstances:

  • Assigned Technicians: When a booking is confirmed, your first name, phone number, and service address are shared with the assigned technician solely to perform the work.
  • Legal & Safety Compliance: When required by applicable Cambodian laws, regulations, or valid legal processes.

5 Data Retention & Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All communication with our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols. We retain your booking and account records only for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal requirements.

6 Account & Data Deletion Requests

In compliance with Google Play and Apple App Store policies, you have the full right to delete your account and request complete removal of your personal information from our active databases.

How to request account & data deletion:
1. Inside the Seva24 Mobile App: Go to Profile > Edit > tap the Delete Person icon on the top-right app bar > type confirmation text into the pop-up > confirm deletion.
2. Online Web Request: Visit our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page to submit an online deletion request.
3. By Direct Contact: Call our customer support at 078 222 876 or reach out to DGTALIZE PARTNERS Co., Ltd. at our Contact Page.

7 Children's Privacy

Seva24 does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete it.
