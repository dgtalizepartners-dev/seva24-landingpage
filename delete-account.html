Important Notice: Deleting your Seva24 account is permanent. Once completed, your profile, authentication credentials, registered phone number, service address history, and preferences will be permanently wiped from our active systems.

1 Method 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

If you have the Seva24 app installed on your smartphone, you can instantly delete your account at any time:

  1. Open the Seva24 application on your mobile device and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap on Edit (Edit Profile).
  4. On the top-right of the app bar, tap the Delete Person icon.
  5. A confirmation dialog will pop up. Type the confirmation text into the input field and confirm to permanently delete your account.

2 Method 2: Submit Web Deletion Request
